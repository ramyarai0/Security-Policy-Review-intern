# RECOMMENDED POLICY IMPROVEMENTS

Based on the security gaps and risks identified during the policy review, the following improvements are recommended. These recommendations focus on strengthening preventive, detective and corrective security controls while making policy requirements more specific, measurable and easier to verify.

### 10.1 Access Control and Least Privilegeḥ

- Define joiner-mover-leaver account procedures.

- Grant only role-required access.

- Require approval for privileged access.

- Review privileged and sensitive access quarterly.

- Record review evidence and remove unnecessary access.

### 10.2 Authentication and MFA

- Require MFA for email, cloud applications, VPN and administrator accounts.

- Use secure password/authentication practices.

- Prohibit password sharing.

- Define secure account-recovery procedures.

- Review MFA coverage periodically.

### 10.3 Data Protection

- Classify information as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.

- Define permitted storage and sharing methods.

- Protect sensitive data during transmission and storage.

- Define secure disposal requirements.

### 10.4 Endpoint and Patch Management

- Maintain an inventory of devices/software.

- Define severity-based patch deadlines.

- Use centrally managed endpoint protection.

- Track exceptions and overdue patches.

### 10.5 Incident Response

- Define incident severity levels.

- Assign response roles and contacts.

- Specify reporting and escalation timelines.

- Maintain incident records.

- Conduct post-incident reviews and update controls.

### 10.6 Backup and Recovery

- Define backup frequency and retention.

- Protect backups from unauthorized modification.

- Maintain appropriate isolated copies for critical data.

- Perform documented restore tests.

- Record restoration results and corrective actions.

### 10.7 Security Awareness

- Train employees during onboarding.

- Provide recurring refresher training.

- Cover phishing, social engineering, suspicious attachments and reporting.

- Record completion and evaluate awareness results.

### 10.8 Vulnerability Management

- Conduct regular vulnerability scanning.

- Prioritize by severity and business impact.

- Assign owners and deadlines.

- Track vulnerabilities to closure.

- Document approved exceptions.

### 10.9 Third-Party Security

- Assess important vendors before onboarding.

- Include security and incident-notification requirements where appropriate.

- Limit vendor access.

- Review vendor access periodically.

- Maintain vendor security records.

### 10.10 Remote Access

Require employees to use only approved remote-access methods.

Require MFA for remote connections.

Ensure remote devices meet organizational security requirements.

Disable inactive or unnecessary remote-access accounts.

Review remote-access permissions periodically.

### 10.11 Policy Governance

- Assign a policy owner.

- Record version, approval and review dates.

- Review at least annually and after significant incidents or changes.

- Communicate approved policy changes.
