# IDENTIFIED SECURITY GAPS

The security policy review identified several gaps that could affect the organization’s ability to protect its systems, data and users. These gaps were identified by comparing the assumed policy requirements with practical security controls and verification needs. The following table summarizes the major security gaps, their potential impact, priority level and recommended actions for improvement.

| ID | Gap | Potential Impact | Priority | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G-01 | MFA not explicitly mandatory | Credential compromise | Critical | Make MFA mandatory for high-risk services |
| G-02 | No formal access-review schedule | Privilege misuse | High | Quarterly sensitive/privileged access review |
| G-03 | Patch deadlines unclear | Exploitation of known vulnerabilities | Critical | Create severity-based patch SLAs |
| G-04 | Incident escalation unclear | Delayed response | High | Define severity, contacts and escalation timelines |
| G-05 | Backup testing undefined | Recovery failure | High | Perform and document restoration tests |
| G-06 | Data classification missing | Improper data handling | Medium | Introduce data classification and handling rules |
| G-07 | Security awareness training frequency and requirements are unclear | Phishing/social engineering risk | Medium | Define recurring awareness activities |
| G-08 | Vendor controls limited | Third-party compromise | High | Add vendor assessment and access-review controls |
| G-09 | Remote access requirements are broadly defined | Unauthorized remote access | Medium | Define approved methods and MFA |
| G-10 | Policy review cycle unclear | Outdated controls | Medium | Assign owner and annual review cycle |
| G-11 | Endpoint patching and protection requirements lack measurable | Increased malware and system compromise risk | High | Define patch SLAs and verify endpoint protection coverage |
| G-12 | Vulnerability remediation timelines are not defined | Prolonged exposure to exploitable vulnerabilities | High | Establish severity-based remediation timelines and track vulnerabilities to closure |
