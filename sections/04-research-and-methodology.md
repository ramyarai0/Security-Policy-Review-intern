# 4. RESEARCH AND METHODOLOGY

### 4.1 Research Approach

I followed a research-based and practical policy-review approach. First, I studied the assumed policy requirements. I then grouped them into major cybersecurity control areas, compared the level of detail available in each area, identified gaps, assessed risks and prepared improvement actions. Finally, I validated the recommendations against hypothetical scenarios.

### 4.2 Data Collection

For this academic exercise, the primary source was a hypothetical organizational security policy. Supporting cybersecurity concepts were organized around recognized areas such as access control, authentication, data protection, incident response, backup, vulnerability management, security awareness, and third-party risk.

### 4.3 Data Analysis

The policy was analyzed using a gap-analysis method. For each control area, I considered whether the requirement was present, whether it contained a specific action, whether responsibility was identifiable, whether a review or testing frequency was defined, and whether evidence of compliance could be produced.

### 4.4 Practical Review Approach

To make the policy review practical and measurable, each security control was examined not only for its presence but also for how it could be implemented and verified. The review considered the specific action required, the evidence that could demonstrate compliance, the responsible area, and the appropriate review frequency. This approach helped convert general policy requirements into practical security controls that can be monitored and reviewed.

| Step | Action Performed | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify the policy control area. | Control-area list |
| 2 | Read the requirement and identify what is missing or unclear. | Observation |
| 3 | Convert the observation into a specific verification action. | Action-oriented checklist |
| 4 | Assess likelihood and impact. | Risk rating |
| 5 | Recommend a control improvement. | Recommendation |
| 6 | Test the recommendation against a hypothetical scenario. | Validation result |
| 7 | Assign priority and implementation timing. | Roadmap |
