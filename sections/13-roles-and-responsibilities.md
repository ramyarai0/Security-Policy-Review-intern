# ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Effective implementation of the recommended security improvements requires clearly assigned responsibilities. The following roles are identified based on their involvement in policy approval, technical implementation, employee management, access control and ongoing policy maintenance. The evidence/record column identifies the documentation that can demonstrate completion or accountability.

| Role | Responsibility | Evidence/Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Management | Approve security policies, priorities, resources and major risk-treatment decisions. | Approved policy/meeting record |
| IT/Security Team | Implement security controls, monitor systems, manage vulnerabilities and support incident response. | Security reports/tickets |
| HR | Support onboarding/offboarding and policy acknowledgement. | HR/IT records |
| System Owners | Approve access and review system-specific security requirements. | Access approvals/reviews |
| Employees | Follow policy, protect credentials and report suspicious activity. | Training and incident-report records |
| Vendors/Third Parties | Follow contractual security requirements, maintain appropriate access controls and report relevant security incidents. | Vendor assessment/access records |
| Policy Owner | Maintain policy versions, coordinate periodic reviews, track approvals and monitor completion of improvement actions. | Policy register/version history and review records |
