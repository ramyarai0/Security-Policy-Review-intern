# 14. CHALLENGES FACED

The following challenges were encountered while conducting the security policy review:

Determining the appropriate security-policy areas to include in the baseline for a medium-sized organization.

Converting broad policy statements into specific and measurable security actions.

Establishing clear relationships between identified policy gaps and realistic cybersecurity risks.

Designing an action-oriented checklist that includes verification evidence and review frequency rather than only Yes/No questions.

Developing meaningful validation scenarios without access to a real organization's confidential systems, security infrastructure or internal policy documents.

Maintaining consistency between identified gaps, risk priorities, recommended improvements and implementation activities.
