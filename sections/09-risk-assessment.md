# 9. RISK ASSESSMENT

Risk priority is estimated using likelihood and impact. The scale is intentionally simple for this academic review so that the organization can prioritize practical policy improvements.

| Risk ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Priority | Recommended Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Credential compromise due to weak authentication | High | High | Critical | Enforce MFA and secure authentication |
| R-02 | Exploitation of unpatched systems | High | High | Critical | Define patch SLAs and track overdue remediation |
| R-03 | Delayed incident response | Medium | High | High | Define escalation roles and timelines |
| R-04 | Privilege misuse | Medium | High | High | Least privilege and quarterly access review |
| R-05 | Backup failure | Medium | High | High | Document and test restoration |
| R-06 | Third-party security incident | Medium | High | High | Vendor assessment and access controls |
| R-07 | Phishing/social engineering | High | Medium | High | Recurring security awareness |
| R-08 | Improper sensitive-data handling | Medium | Medium | Medium | Data classification and handling rules |
