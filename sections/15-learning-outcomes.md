# 15. LEARNING OUTCOMES

The following learning outcomes were achieved through the completion of the security policy review:

I learned how to review a security policy systematically rather than only reading it for general content.

I learned how to identify policy gaps and connect them to realistic cybersecurity risks.

I learned why security-policy requirements should clearly define actions, responsibilities and review frequency.

I learned how an action-oriented checklist can be used to support evidence-based security reviews.

I learned how scenario-based validation can help determine whether recommended policy controls are practical.

I learned how to prioritize security improvements using likelihood and impact.

I improved my understanding of access control, MFA, patch management, backup and recovery, incident response, third-party security and policy governance.

I improved my ability to convert general security requirements into practical, measurable and verifiable recommendations.
